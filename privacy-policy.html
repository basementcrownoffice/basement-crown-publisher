Privacy Policy

Basement Crown Publisher

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Basement Crown Publisher handles information when connecting to Pinterest through the Pinterest API.

Purpose of the Application

Basement Crown Publisher is an internal application used by Basement Crown to manage and publish content to its own Pinterest business account. The application is intended for personal business use and is not offered as a service to customers or other Pinterest users.

Information Accessed

When authorized through Pinterest, the application may access information associated with the authenticated Basement Crown Pinterest account, including Pins, Boards, and information necessary to create, publish, organize, and manage Pinterest content.

How Information Is Used

Information accessed through the Pinterest API is used solely to operate Basement Crown's own Pinterest publishing workflow. This may include creating Pins, publishing content, scheduling publishing activities, selecting Boards, and verifying published content.

Sharing of Information

Basement Crown Publisher does not sell, rent, or share Pinterest account data with advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties.

Information may be processed by service providers only when technically necessary to operate the application or its hosting infrastructure, subject to applicable privacy and security requirements.

Data Retention

Information obtained through the Pinterest API is retained only when necessary for the operation of the publishing workflow, troubleshooting, security, or legal obligations. Data that is no longer required will be deleted when reasonably practicable.

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect authentication credentials and information handled by Basement Crown Publisher from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse.

User Control

Access to Pinterest data is authorized by the Pinterest account owner. Authorization may be revoked through Pinterest account settings where supported by Pinterest.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of Basement Crown Publisher or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or Basement Crown Publisher, please contact Basement Crown through the Basement Crown Etsy shop.
