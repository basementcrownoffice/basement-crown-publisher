Basement Crown Publisher

Basement Crown Publisher is an internal content publishing application developed for Basement Crown, an independent wall art and poster business.

Purpose of the application

The application helps the owner of Basement Crown prepare and publish visual content from the Basement Crown product catalog to the official Basement Crown Pinterest business account.

Basement Crown Publisher is an internal business tool. It is not offered as a public service to third-party Pinterest users.

How Basement Crown Publisher uses the Pinterest API

The application uses the official Pinterest API to connect the authorized Basement Crown Pinterest business account and support the publishing and management of Basement Crown Pins and Boards.

Content published through the application consists of Basement Crown's own product artwork, product information, descriptions and destination links.

The application does not scrape Pinterest and is not designed to collect data from unrelated Pinterest users.

Authorization and account access

Pinterest access is performed through Pinterest's official authorization process. The application only accesses Pinterest information and functionality permitted by the account owner and the scopes granted through the Pinterest API.

Access can be revoked by disconnecting the application from the authorized Pinterest account.

Data and privacy

Information about how Basement Crown Publisher handles Pinterest data, retention, security, revocation and privacy is available in our Privacy Policy.
