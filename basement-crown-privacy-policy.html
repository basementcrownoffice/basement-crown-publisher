Privacy Policy

Basement Crown Publisher

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Basement Crown Publisher accesses, uses, stores, protects, and deletes information when connecting to Pinterest through the official Pinterest API.

Purpose of the Application

Basement Crown Publisher is an internal content publishing application used by Basement Crown, an independent wall art and poster business, to manage and publish Basement Crown content to its own Pinterest business account.

The application is an internal business tool and is not offered as a service to customers or unrelated Pinterest users.

Pinterest Data Accessed

When authorized by the Basement Crown Pinterest account owner, Basement Crown Publisher may access Pinterest data and functionality made available through the Pinterest API and permitted by the authorization scopes granted to the application.

This may include information relating to the authenticated Pinterest account, Pins, Boards, Pin publishing status, and other information necessary to create, publish, organize, manage, and verify Basement Crown content on Pinterest.

Basement Crown Publisher is not designed to scrape Pinterest or collect data from unrelated Pinterest users.

How Pinterest Data Is Used

Pinterest data is used solely to operate Basement Crown's own Pinterest content publishing workflow. This may include preparing and creating Pins, publishing Basement Crown content, organizing content into Boards, managing publishing activities, and verifying the status of published content.

Pinterest data is not used for advertising profiles, data brokerage, or profiling unrelated Pinterest users.

Sharing and Service Providers

Basement Crown Publisher does not sell Pinterest data and does not provide Pinterest data to advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties.

Limited information may be processed by technical service providers when necessary to operate the application's infrastructure, hosting, storage, or content delivery functions. Such processing is limited to what is technically necessary to operate Basement Crown Publisher.

No Sale, Resale, or Redistribution of Pinterest Data

Pinterest content and Pinterest-derived data accessed through Basement Crown Publisher are not sold, resold, licensed, or redistributed to third parties. The application uses Pinterest data only for the authorized Basement Crown Pinterest publishing workflow.

Data Retention

Pinterest data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the application, maintain the publishing workflow, verify publishing activity, troubleshoot technical issues, maintain security, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Data that is no longer required for these purposes is deleted or securely removed when reasonably practicable.

Revocation and Disconnection

The Pinterest account owner may revoke Basement Crown Publisher's access to Pinterest through the applicable Pinterest account settings or authorization controls.

If Pinterest access is revoked or the Pinterest account is disconnected, Basement Crown Publisher will stop accessing Pinterest data through that authorization. Pinterest data retained solely for the operation of the connection will be deleted when it is no longer required, subject only to limited information that may need to be retained temporarily for security, technical troubleshooting, or applicable legal obligations.

Data Security

Basement Crown Publisher uses reasonable technical and organizational security measures designed to protect Pinterest data and authentication credentials against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse.

Access credentials and API tokens are restricted to the application owner and the systems necessary to operate the application. Sensitive authentication credentials are not intentionally exposed through the application's public website.

How to Revoke Access

The Pinterest account owner can revoke the application's authorization using the applicable connected-app, permissions, or account settings provided by Pinterest. Revoking authorization prevents Basement Crown Publisher from continuing to access Pinterest through the revoked authorization.

For assistance with disconnecting Basement Crown Publisher or requesting the deletion of Pinterest data held by the application, contact: basementcrownoffice@gmail.com.

Privacy Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or questions about how Basement Crown Publisher handles Pinterest data, contact: basementcrownoffice@gmail.com.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of Basement Crown Publisher, its use of the Pinterest API, or applicable requirements change. The current version will remain publicly available on this page.

Pinterest Disclaimer
